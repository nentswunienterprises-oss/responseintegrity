Expected Answer
To verify where a newly entering topic should begin by running the current-phase verification block and moving only to an adjacent phase when the score band requires it.

Q3
What is the purpose of a Training drill?

Expected Answer
To condition the capability at an already placed topic-state by running the live phase's full training sets and constraints exactly as defined.

Fail Answer
To decide the starting phase
To choose questions freely
To verify a new tutor

Q4

Expected Answer
To check whether an inherited or continuity-sensitive topic-state still holds without restarting placement or running a normal full training cycle.

Fail Answer
To repeat Intro
To teach before a handover
To replace the stored state with tutor judgment

SECTION 2: LIVE TRAINING LANES

Q5
How is Clarity Training structured, and which part is not scored evidence?

Expected Answer
It uses one Modeling preparation followed by Identification and Light Apply. Modeling builds the mental map and is not scored proof of independent Clarity; the later reps generate evidence.

Fail Answer
All three parts prove independent Clarity
Clarity has recognition only
Modeling is the final assessment

Q6
What support distinctions must be preserved across Structured Execution sets?

Expected Answer
Required Structure allows minimal support while step order is established. Independent Execution and Variation Control allow no tutor support; Variation Control also changes the surface form.

Fail Answer
First-step help is allowed in every set
The tutor may prompt whenever the student hesitates
All three sets use the same support

Q7
What support distinctions must be preserved across Controlled Discomfort sets?

Expected Answer
Controlled Entry allows minimal support, No Rescue allows first-step-only support, and Repeat Exposure allows no support while difficulty remains active.

Fail Answer
Limited guidance is allowed in every set
No Rescue means no difficulty
Repeat Exposure allows a rescue after each attempt

Q8
How do the three Time Pressure Stability sets change the pressure condition?

Expected Answer
Structure Under Timer introduces a light timer, Repeated Timed Execution repeats the timer condition, and Full Constraint applies the tightest defined constraint. All preserve no support and method over speed.

Fail Answer
They only ask the student to work faster
The tutor chooses any timer each rep
Full Constraint permits shortcuts

Q9
Why is a repetition not merely another maths question?

Expected Answer
It is another controlled exposure and evidence opportunity used to see whether the intended response recurs under the set's condition rather than appearing once by luck, cueing, or familiarity.

Fail Answer
Repetitions are for extra practice
One correct answer already proves stability
They fill the planned session time

Q10
What must preparation contain before a drill starts?

Expected Answer
The exact problems or reps, correct phase fit, delivery order, active timer or difficulty condition, support boundary, and any adjacent verification coverage required by the drill mode.

Fail Answer
Only the topic name
The tutor can improvise the questions live
Preparation means explaining the method beforehand

SECTION 3: PRESSURE SCENARIOS

Scenario 1
During Clarity Identification, the student begins solving and the Specialist lets the rep continue because the answer is correct. What was lost?

Expected Answer
Identification is recognition without solving. Letting execution replace recognition destroys the set's evidentiary question and the rep cannot prove the intended Clarity behaviour.

Fail Answer
Nothing because the answer was correct
The rep became stronger evidence
Solving is always the best proof

Scenario 2
During Structured Execution Variation Control, the Specialist confirms the first step on every rep. Can the results prove independent transfer?

Expected Answer
No. Variation Control requires no support and a changed form. Repeated first-step confirmation contaminates independence and transfer evidence.

Fail Answer
Yes, because only the first step was helped
Yes, if the rest was correct
Support level does not affect transfer

Scenario 3
During Controlled Discomfort Repeat Exposure, the student asks for help and the Specialist gives a hint to maintain momentum. What should happen?

Expected Answer
Repeat Exposure has no support. The hint is a rescue that invalidates independent evidence for that rep; record the actual dependency and follow the authorised recovery or next action.

Fail Answer
Hints are allowed because the phase is difficult
Do not record the hint
Mark partial independence

Scenario 4
During Full Constraint, the student finishes quickly by guessing and skipping structure. How should the drill be read?

Expected Answer
The timed condition exposed instability. Speed does not replace structure or completion integrity, so the relevant fields must be logged weak even if time was met.

Fail Answer
The drill passed because the student finished
Reward the speed
Ignore method once the full timer is active
