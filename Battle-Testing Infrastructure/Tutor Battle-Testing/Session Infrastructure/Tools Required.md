Response Integrity-OS BATTLE TEST: TOOLS REQUIRED

SECTION 1: COMPULSORY DELIVERY KIT

Q1
What three tools remain compulsory in the live Response Integrity delivery kit?

Expected Answer
A smartphone, mini ring light, and earphones or headset.

Fail Answer
A laptop, digital whiteboard, and headset
Only a phone
Whatever equipment the Specialist already owns

Q2
What is the smartphone's operating role across a Response Integrity session?

Expected Answer
It is the live student-call camera and display. In Clarity Modelling, the rear camera and attached ring light face the Specialist's work. After Clarity, the phone is placed vertically in selfie mode so the Specialist can remain connected to the student while observing the student's response.

Fail Answer
It stays in one top-down angle for every drill
It is mainly for messaging
It replaces the Response Integrity platform

Q3
Why is the mini ring light compulsory?

Expected Answer
It protects visibility. In Modelling it must light the notebook and hands clearly without glare or obstructive shadows. In Observation it supports a clear live interaction view.

Fail Answer
It is decorative
It only makes the Specialist look professional
Normal room light always guarantees the required condition

Q4
What is the laptop or computer used for during the live session?

Expected Answer
It is the Response Integrity operating surface. The Specialist runs the drill and records concrete observations and evidence there while the phone remains on the student call.

Fail Answer
It replaces the phone camera
It is used only for entertainment between reps
Observations should be written from memory after the session

Q5
Why are earphones or a headset compulsory?

Expected Answer
They protect clear two-way audio so instructions and student responses can be heard reliably without avoidable feedback or distraction.

Fail Answer
They are only for privacy
They are optional when the Specialist speaks loudly
They play lesson recordings

SECTION 2: MODE SELECTION AND SETUP

Q6
When is Modelling mode used, and what must the phone show?

Expected Answer
It is used in the Clarity phase Modelling set to show and explain. The rear camera and attached ring light face the Specialist's notebook and hands so the student can see the method being executed from a top-down learner perspective.

Fail Answer
It is the default camera mode for every phase
The camera should stay on the Specialist's face
The Specialist can explain verbally without showing the work

Q7
When is Observation mode used, and what is the Specialist doing?

Expected Answer
It is used in all phases after Clarity. The phone is vertical in selfie mode for the live student connection while the student executes. The Specialist observes the response and records concrete observations on the laptop, intervening only when the drill requires it.

Fail Answer
The Specialist keeps demonstrating examples throughout the session
The phone stays aimed at the Specialist's notebook
The Specialist waits until after the session to reconstruct observations

Q8
Is a specific gooseneck phone holder compulsory?

Expected Answer
No specific gooseneck product is compulsory. A gooseneck is the standard reference because it creates stable hands-free positioning and repeatable switching between Modelling and Observation. Any equivalent setup must preserve the same stability and must not block the cameras or ring light.

Fail Answer
Any unstable hand-held angle is equivalent
The holder may cover the rear cameras if the light still works
The gooseneck brand itself is what makes evidence valid

Q9
What must the Specialist verify before the first rep?

Expected Answer
The active drill mode is correct, the phone is stable, the required view is visible and in focus, the ring light is aligned without glare, two-way audio works, the Response Integrity drill is open on the laptop, observation fields are ready, and required materials are available.

Fail Answer
Only that the video call opens
The setup can be fixed after several reps
The student is responsible for checking the Specialist's condition

Q10
Why does the correct camera mode matter to evidence integrity?

Expected Answer
The camera mode determines what the Specialist and student can validly see during that drill condition. Modelling must expose the Specialist's execution; post-Clarity work must preserve student execution and Specialist observation. Using the wrong mode can change the condition, hide behaviour, or turn observation into demonstration.

Fail Answer
Camera mode only changes appearance
The same view is valid for every drill
Evidence is unaffected when the wrong behaviour is being shown or observed

SECTION 3: PRESSURE SCENARIOS

Scenario 1
Structured Execution begins after Clarity, but the Specialist leaves the phone aimed at the notebook and keeps demonstrating examples while the student mostly watches. Is the condition valid?

Expected Answer
No. The session should be in Observation mode. The student must execute while the Specialist observes the response and logs concrete behaviour on the laptop. Continuing Specialist demonstration changes the condition and cannot prove independent execution.

Fail Answer
Yes, because a clear notebook view is always the safest setup
Yes, if the Specialist explains the method well
Keep demonstrating until the student looks confident

Scenario 2
During the Clarity Modelling set, the Specialist leaves the phone in selfie mode and explains the solution verbally without showing the written execution. Is the Modelling condition valid?

Expected Answer
No. Clarity Modelling requires the camera and light to face the Specialist's work so the student can see the method being executed and explained. Restore the top-down work view before continuing the Modelling set.

Fail Answer
Yes, because the student can hear the explanation
Yes, if the Specialist's face is clearly visible
Skip the visual demonstration and score the student's reaction

Scenario 3
The phone is correctly in Observation mode, but the Specialist's laptop is not ready. They complete the reps and plan to fill in the observation fields later from memory. What is wrong?

Expected Answer
Live observation integrity is lost. The drill and observation fields must be ready before the reps begin. Missing behaviour must not be reconstructed later from memory as if it were captured live.

Fail Answer
Memory is enough if the Specialist is experienced
Complete the session and estimate the missing fields
Ask the student afterward what happened

Scenario 4
In Modelling, the holder partly covers the rear camera and the ring light creates glare over part of the written method. Does having the correct equipment mean the condition is valid?

Expected Answer
No. The equipment must produce the intended observable condition. Reposition the phone, holder, and light until the rear camera has a clear view and the full method is readable without glare.

Fail Answer
Yes, because all required tools are present
The student can infer the hidden work
Only the final answer needs to be visible

FINAL TEST

FINAL QUESTION
What is the Specialist's tools standard?

Expected Answer
Arrive with the compulsory smartphone, mini ring light, and earphones or headset; keep the Response Integrity platform ready on a laptop or computer; establish a stable phone position; use camera-and-light-facing-work for the Clarity Modelling set; switch to vertical selfie Observation mode after Clarity; verify visibility, audio, and logging readiness before evidence collection; and stop whenever the required condition becomes unobservable.

Fail Answer
Use one camera angle for the whole session
Join the call with whatever is available
Fix missing observations after the session
