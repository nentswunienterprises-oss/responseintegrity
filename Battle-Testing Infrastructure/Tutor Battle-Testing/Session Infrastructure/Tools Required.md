Q1
What three tools are compulsory for the live Response Integrity delivery setup?

Expected Answer
A smartphone, mini ring light, and earphones.

Fail Answer

Whatever equipment the tutor already owns

Q2
What is the smartphone's primary role in the session?

Expected Answer
It provides the stable top-down camera view of the Specialist's real handwritten work so the student can follow the method from the learner's perspective.

Fail Answer
It is mainly for messaging
It records attendance
It replaces written working with an app

Q3
Why is the mini ring light compulsory?

Expected Answer
It keeps the page and written method clearly visible and reduces hand or pen shadows that would obstruct the student's view.

Fail Answer
It makes the tutor look professional
It is decorative
Normal room light is always enough

Q4
Why are earphones compulsory?

Expected Answer
They keep tutor audio clear and reduce distraction or feedback so instructions and student responses can be heard reliably.

Fail Answer

Q5

Q6
Is a gooseneck phone holder compulsory?

Expected Answer
No. It is optional but highly recommended because it stabilises the top-down angle, prevents arm fatigue, and makes the setup repeatable.

Fail Answer

Q7
What must the Specialist verify before the session begins?

Expected Answer
The full working area is visible and in focus, lighting removes obstructive shadows, the phone is stable, audio works both ways, earphones are connected, and required materials are ready.

Fail Answer
Only that the video call opens
Equipment can be adjusted after the first drill
The student is responsible for checking the setup

Q8
Why does setup quality matter to evidence integrity?

Expected Answer
The Specialist and student must be able to see and hear the behaviour being trained. Obscured work or unclear audio makes the condition unreliable and can make observations impossible to defend.

Fail Answer
It only affects appearance
Evidence is unchanged if the page cannot be seen
Setup has no link to the methodology

Q9

Expected Answer
Stop and restore the valid view before using the rep as evidence. Do not score behaviour that could not be observed reliably.

Fail Answer
Continue and infer the missing steps
Ask the student whether it looked correct
Score from the final answer

Q10
Does Response Integrity require expensive specialist software or gadgets?

Expected Answer
No. The method deliberately uses a simple physical setup. The compulsory tools must meet the visibility and audio standard, but complex specialist software is not required.

Fail Answer
Yes, specialist software is essential

SECTION 3: PRESSURE SCENARIOS

Scenario 1
The Specialist starts a live drill although the student can see only half the page. They plan to explain any hidden work verbally. Is the condition valid?

Expected Answer
No. The top-down written method must be visible. Starting with an obstructed view invalidates delivery and any evidence dependent on seeing the working.

Fail Answer
Yes, verbal explanation compensates
Yes, if the final answer is visible
Continue and fix it next session

Scenario 2
The Specialist's audio repeatedly cuts out, but they complete and score the student's spoken recognition reps. What is wrong?

Expected Answer
The spoken evidence was not reliably observable. Repair the audio condition and rerun affected reps rather than inventing or guessing what was said.

Fail Answer
Use the tutor's best judgment
Score from facial expression
Audio quality is not part of the drill

Scenario 3
The ring light creates glare that hides the written steps. Does having the compulsory tool mean the standard is met?

Expected Answer
No. The tool must produce the intended condition. Reposition the light until the full method is clear; possession without usable visibility is not compliance.

Fail Answer
Yes, because the ring light is present
Glare is the student's problem
Turn the camera away from the page

Scenario 4
The Specialist does not own a gooseneck holder but can maintain a stable, unobstructed top-down view throughout the session. May the session proceed?

Expected Answer
Yes. The holder is recommended, not compulsory. The required delivery condition is stable visible work with the compulsory phone, ring light, and earphones.

Fail Answer
No, the holder is mandatory
Yes, and the ring light can also be omitted
Only a laptop view matters
