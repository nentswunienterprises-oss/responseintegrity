Response Integrity-OS BATTLE TEST: LOGGING SYSTEM

SECTION 1: EVIDENCE PURPOSE

Q1
What is the purpose of logging in Response Integrity?

Expected Answer
To preserve a trustworthy rep-by-rep record of what the student actually did so the system can score the response, determine the next action, and support defensible downstream reporting.

Fail Answer
To write a session summary
To show parents that progress happened
To record tutor impressions

Q2
What is the difference between an observation and an interpretation?

Expected Answer
An observation states visible or audible behaviour, such as an eight-second pause or asking for the next step. An interpretation assigns an unproven meaning, such as panic, laziness, or low confidence.

Fail Answer
They are the same
An experienced tutor can record the psychological cause
Interpretation is more useful than behaviour

Q3
Why must evidence remain attached to its set and repetition?

Expected Answer
Because the set defines the condition being tested and each repetition is a separate evidence opportunity. Removing that lineage makes the behaviour impossible to interpret against the intended constraint.

Fail Answer
Only the final phase total matters
The tutor will remember the context
Set and rep numbers are administration

SECTION 2: RECORDING DISCIPLINE

Q4
How should a Specialist choose an observation option after a rep?

Expected Answer
Choose the raw option that most closely matches the behaviour that actually occurred. Do not improve, soften, average, or reinterpret the behaviour to create a preferred result.

Fail Answer
Choose the option that reflects potential
Round up when the student nearly did it
Pick the option that keeps progression moving

Q5
A student asks, "Do I divide here?" What may the Specialist record, and what may they not conclude from that alone?

Expected Answer
Record the observable request for step confirmation in the matching field. Do not conclude from that single behaviour that the student lacks confidence, panicked, or does not understand the whole topic.

Fail Answer
Record that the student panicked
Record low confidence
Record that Clarity failed completely

Q6
Why can a correct final answer still produce weak evidence?

Expected Answer
Because the response may have depended on prompts, rescue, guessing, broken structure, or an invalid condition. RI scores the behaviour around execution, not answer correctness alone.

Fail Answer
A correct answer is always clear evidence
Only marks matter
Correctness cancels any assistance

Q7
A student gives a wrong answer but stays controlled, identifies the type, and states an independent first step. How should this be logged?

Expected Answer
Log each observed field truthfully. The answer can be wrong while first-step control or discomfort tolerance shows partial or clear evidence; do not convert the whole rep into one global judgment.

Fail Answer
Mark every field weak because the answer is wrong
Ignore the wrong answer
Write that the student improved

Q8
The Specialist supplied repeated prompts, but selects "independent" because the student eventually completed the question. What integrity breach occurred?

Expected Answer
Assisted performance was recorded as independent evidence. The selected option does not match the condition or behaviour, so the score and any resulting movement are invalid.

Fail Answer
No breach because the student completed it
Prompts do not affect evidence
Independence means finishing eventually

SECTION 3: SYSTEM OUTPUT AND CLAIMS

Q9
If a behaviour was not seen or heard, may the Specialist log it because it is probably true?

Expected Answer
No. If it was not observed, it must not be logged as fact. The Specialist records evidence, not a likely story.

Fail Answer
Yes, when the tutor knows the student
Yes, if it makes the log complete
Use professional judgment to fill gaps

Q10
Who determines phase placement or movement after the observations are submitted?

Expected Answer
The system applies the deterministic scoring and movement rules. The Specialist preserves the condition, selects truthful observations, and follows the system output rather than manufacturing a conclusion.

Fail Answer
The Specialist decides from the total session
The parent and tutor agree
The score is only advice

SECTION 4: PRESSURE SCENARIOS

Scenario 1
The student pauses for six seconds, looks back at the question, and then starts correctly. The Specialist writes, "Student panicked but recovered." What should replace that log?

Expected Answer
Record the observable sequence: six-second pause, re-read or re-orientation, then a correct start. "Panicked" is an unsupported psychological inference.

Fail Answer
Keep the note because it sounds accurate
Write "low confidence"
Remove the pause because the start was correct

Scenario 2
The Specialist forgot to log two reps and recreates the options later from memory so the set can be submitted. Is that acceptable?

Expected Answer
No. Retrospectively manufacturing missing rep evidence corrupts the record. The missing evidence must be declared and the affected condition rerun or handled through the authorised recovery path.

Fail Answer
Yes, if it is the same day
Yes, experienced tutors remember
Fill in average options

Scenario 3
The system output says hold, but the Specialist edits the narrative to say the student is ready to move because the parent expects progress. What failed?

Expected Answer
The Specialist replaced evidence and system authority with a desired claim. Logs and reports must reflect the actual observations and deterministic result.

Fail Answer
Good parent communication
The narrative can override the score
Progress language is harmless

Scenario 4
One rep is clear, one partial, and one weak under the same set constraint. What is the correct logging action?

Expected Answer
Record all three reps separately and exactly as observed. Do not average them into a cleaner story; repeated evidence exists to reveal stability or drift across opportunities.

Fail Answer
Log the best rep
Log the final rep only
Choose partial for all three

FINAL TEST

FINAL QUESTION
What is the Specialist's responsibility in the evidence chain?

Expected Answer
Create the valid condition, observe without inventing, record every rep truthfully in its set context, and let the system turn that evidence into state movement and downstream claims.

Fail Answer
Write a helpful session summary
Decide what the evidence means
Make the student look successful
