Response Integrity-OS BATTLE TEST: HANDOVER VERIFICATION

SECTION 1: CONTINUITY PURPOSE

Q1
When is a Handover Verification session required?

Expected Answer
When an already active student moves to a replacement Specialist and continuity of the inherited topic-state must be verified before normal Training continues.

Fail Answer
At the end of every session
Whenever a student needs independence
Only when the parent requests it

Q2
What state must the replacement Specialist inherit and review?

Expected Answer
The student's active topic, current phase, current stability, prior evidence and history, assigned next action, and active constraints.

Fail Answer
Only the topic name
Only the latest mark
Nothing; the new Specialist starts fresh

Q3
What is the central purpose of Handover Verification?

Expected Answer
To verify whether inherited topic-state is still trustworthy enough to continue from without erasing history, re-placing the student by instinct, or beginning Training before continuity is established.

Fail Answer
To onboard the new Specialist by teaching the student
To place the student from scratch
To run a normal Training session

Q4
Why is Handover not a new Intro session or another Training drill?

Expected Answer
Intro establishes entry truth where placement does not yet exist. Training deliberately changes capability. Handover starts from inherited truth and only verifies whether that truth still holds under the inherited phase conditions.

Fail Answer
They are equivalent because the Specialist is new
Every Specialist needs their own diagnosis
Handover should train the student until the old state looks correct

SECTION 2: EVIDENCE-DRIVEN VERIFICATION

Q5
What should happen before the live continuity check begins?

Expected Answer
Review the inherited state and evidence, confirm the continuity-check booking, prepare a small reserve bank of phase-matched problems, preserve the inherited phase constraints, and understand that the reserve bank is not a fixed completion target.

Fail Answer
Prepare exactly three problems that must all be completed
Prepare a full Training lesson
Choose a new phase from the parent report

Q6
How does the replacement Specialist run Handover?

Expected Answer
Present one clean continuity opportunity at a time, record the concrete phase-defining behavior, and let the system decide whether another comparable opportunity is needed. Handover ends when evidence is sufficient to hold, adjust stability, or require targeted re-diagnosis; it does not end because a fixed rep count was completed.

Fail Answer
Run three reps and finish regardless of evidence
Keep drilling until the inherited state is confirmed
Teach between opportunities so the student improves during verification

Q7
What are the permitted outcome families after Handover?

Expected Answer
The inherited state can hold; persistent conditional evidence can produce a bounded same-phase stability adjustment; or confirmed breakdown or unresolved clean evidence can require targeted evidence-complete re-diagnosis.

Fail Answer
The Specialist chooses keep or restart
The student automatically returns to Clarity
Only pass or fail

Q8
When may normal Training reopen after a Specialist change?

Expected Answer
Only after Handover has reached an evidence-sufficient outcome and any required targeted re-diagnosis has cleared. Training must remain closed while inherited truth is unresolved.

Fail Answer
Immediately after the new Specialist is assigned
After one warm-up question
Whenever the Specialist feels comfortable

Q9
Who decides whether the inherited state holds or changes?

Expected Answer
The Response Evidence Model decides from the recorded concrete behavior. The Specialist preserves the condition and records what happened; they do not select phase, stability, recovery, regression, or the preferred outcome.

Fail Answer
The replacement Specialist
The previous Specialist
The parent and Specialist together

Q10
What must Handover evidence preserve?

Expected Answer
Inherited topic, phase and stability; the exact continuity condition; concrete behavior identity for each observed dimension; not-observed or confounded evidence where applicable; recovery history; the evidence-native dimension decisions; resulting state; outcome reason; and next action.

Fail Answer
Only that Handover was completed
A narrative about how the student felt
Only that a final result was produced
The new Specialist's opinion of prior Training

SECTION 3: PRESSURE SCENARIOS

Scenario 1
The new Specialist ignores the inherited Structured Execution state and starts the student at Clarity because "I need to see for myself." What failed?

Expected Answer
They erased continuity and replaced Handover with personal re-placement. The inherited state must be tested through evidence-driven continuity verification. If it becomes untrustworthy, the system routes the topic to targeted re-diagnosis.

Fail Answer
Nothing; a new Specialist should always restart
Clarity is the safest place
Personal assessment is more reliable

Scenario 2
The first clean continuity opportunity shows a phase-defining breakdown. The next two comparable opportunities are clean. May the Specialist immediately call the inherited state recovered or manually lower it?

Expected Answer
No. The system owns the recovery rule and state movement. A real earlier breakdown requires the normal support minimum plus an additional clean supported confirmation before recovery is established. Until the evidence rule resolves, Handover stays open and the Specialist does not train forward or manually change state.

Fail Answer
Yes; two clean attempts erase the first breakdown
Lower the state immediately after the first breakdown
Keep giving problems until the Specialist likes the result

Scenario 3
The system confirms a phase-defining breakdown during Handover. What happens next?

Expected Answer
Stop Handover, freeze the inherited state for continuity purposes, keep normal Training closed, and run targeted evidence-complete re-diagnosis on the flagged topic. Handover itself does not reclassify the phase.

Fail Answer
Restart the full Intro automatically
Ignore it and continue inherited Training
Choose a new phase manually
Keep running Handover opportunities until the inherited phase looks correct

Scenario 4
A continuity opportunity is interrupted and the Specialist had to supply a mathematical step before one dimension could be observed cleanly. How should that dimension be recorded?

Expected Answer
Record it as confounded, or not observed if the behavior never became meaningfully observable. It must count as neither weakness nor strength. The system may request another clean opportunity and, if clean truth remains unresolved at the bounded limit, route to targeted re-diagnosis.

Fail Answer
Choose the closest weak option
Choose the response the student showed after help as independent evidence
Mark it strong because the student eventually completed the step

FINAL QUESTION
What is the replacement Specialist's job in Handover Verification?

Expected Answer
Preserve the student's history and inherited phase conditions, present clean continuity opportunities without turning them into Training, record concrete behavior and ineligible evidence truthfully, and follow the evidence-native hold, stability-adjust, or targeted re-diagnosis result.

Fail Answer
Reassess the student and choose where to begin
Start teaching while learning the student
Trust the old state without verification
Complete a fixed rep count and use personal judgment to decide
