## Distinction

- **Observed performance:** the Specialist sees a student's controlled response, hesitation, collapse, partial recovery or independent completion under the prescribed method and conditions. Interpret the observed evidence normally.
- **Not observed:** the necessary opportunity or material part of the response was not seen or elicited. It is a **limit on evidence**, not the student's capability. Restore visibility, ask for the missing explanation, or obtain a comparable fresh opportunity where possible.
- **Confounded:** a plausible external cue or unauthorized assistance is present, so affected parts of the student's behaviour cannot establish independent capability. Remove the cue and repeat under the original, unchanged method and condition. Preserve any separable, trustworthy observations without falsely certifying the affected ones.
