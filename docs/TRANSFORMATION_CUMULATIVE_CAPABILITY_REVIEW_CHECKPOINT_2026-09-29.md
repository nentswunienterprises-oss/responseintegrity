## Governing standard

The standard is **inevitable comprehensibility**.

Every question was reread as a complete teaching system:

```text
prompt
-> each distractor
-> option-specific feedback
-> correct answer
-> Truth
-> surrounding RI logic
```

The audit asked:

- Does each distractor represent a plausible and specific misconception?
- Does its feedback make that exact mistake clear?
- Does the feedback change Specialist thinking rather than merely say the option is wrong?
- Is every word useful?
- Does feedback teach only the distinction needed at that moment and leave the complete rule to Truth?
- Could the wording be clearer without losing the important distinction?
- Does it sound like RI thinking rather than assessment-writing language?
- Does it leave a reusable mental model rather than wording to memorise?
- Does the item preserve the relevant phase, evidence, support, state-authority and condition boundary?
- Does Retrieval test retained RI thinking without naming the Deep Dive?
- Does Transfer require mixed-situation discrimination without announcing which phase is being tested?

## Manual editorial pass

The full 50-question set was reread bank by bank.

The pass corrected weak or ambiguous wording where the question, distractor, feedback or Truth weakened the intended distinction. Examples include:

- replacing implausible repeated-evidence distractors with real misconceptions about practice volume, repeated correctness and Specialist confidence;
- separating teaching/model-following from independent Clarity evidence more precisely;
- aligning the Clarity manual-progression critical option with the actual prohibited inference;
- replacing psychological/internal-state wording with observable-behaviour language;
- replacing ambiguous timer-failure and learner-freeze wording with explicit condition-vs-learner evidence distinctions;
- rewriting a double-negative transfer item about final-answer correctness into a direct earliest-unsupported-layer discrimination;
- removing assessment/meta wording around High / High Maintenance progression;
- tightening no-help and No Rescue feedback so small hints and full rescue cannot be disguised as independent evidence;
- removing the remaining generic filler phrase in Transfer;
- tightening option-specific teaching where a response was correct in principle but not inevitably comprehensible.

The correct keys were preserved. One critical-fail option was realigned within its existing Clarity critical boundary so the critical choice now matches the actual no-manual-progression violation.

## Retrieval vs Transfer distinction

Retrieval remains a delayed, mixed recall gate. It asks the Specialist to recover RI rules and distinctions after the Mastery context has gone.

Transfer remains a mixed-situation application gate. It uses operating situations that require the Specialist to locate the relevant response layer, authority rule, support boundary or evidence condition without being told which phase is being tested.

The two banks share doctrine, not question wording.
