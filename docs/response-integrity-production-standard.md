# Response Integrity Production Standard

Demand and Capacity are the two halves of the same production system.

## Part I - Demand Production Line

### Purpose
The Demand Production Line exists to transform opportunities into subscribed learners through a structured, measurable, and verifiable production process.

Its output is validated subscriptions.

These subscriptions activate the Pod Economy.

Every Demand Contributor owns a Production Link.

Every opportunity entering through that Production Link is traced from origin to subscription.

New links use `?production=CODE&pipeline=demand` or
`?production=CODE&pipeline=capacity`. Legacy `?affiliate=CODE` links remain
accepted and resolve to the demand pipeline. The production code is retained
on the lead or specialist application; UTM values are supplementary reporting
labels, not the source of attribution truth.

Production Rewards are only unlocked after a verified subscription following a completed trial.

### Demand Production Flow

```text
Opportunity
    ↓
Capture
    ↓
Qualification
    ↓
Trial
    ↓
Subscription
    ↓
Production Reward
```

### Station Definitions

#### 1. Opportunity
A potential learner becomes visible to the system.

Examples:

- Instagram campaign
- School partnership
- Parent referral
- QR code
- Community event
- WhatsApp group
- Affiliate

Output:

Opportunity created.

#### 2. Capture
The opportunity enters the platform through a Production Link.

The system records:

- contributor
- campaign
- source
- ownership
- timestamp

Output:

Lead captured.

#### 3. Qualification
The opportunity is evaluated.

Examples:

- correct grade
- parent interested
- intake available
- geographic fit
- capacity available

Output:

Qualified opportunity.

#### 4. Trial
The learner experiences Response Integrity.

Activities include:

- diagnostic
- trial sessions
- parent reporting
- progress measurement

Output:

Verified educational proof.

#### 5. Subscription
The parent commits.

The learner joins the platform.

This creates a verified conversion.

Output:

Active learner.

#### 6. Production Reward
The platform identifies the owning contributor.

If eligible, Production Reward is released.

If multiple contributors own the Production Link, rewards follow the predefined ownership split.

If organic, no reward is released and the platform retains full production allocation.

For the MVP, an eligible demand contributor receives **R100 per verified
converted student**. Verification requires a completed trial followed by a
paid subscription. This demand reward is separate from the specialist's R750
monthly service share and the platform's R250 production share.

### Success Metric
Demand Production does not optimize for leads.

It optimizes for:

Verified subscriptions.

## Part II - Capacity Production Line

### Purpose
The Capacity Production Line exists to transform opportunities into deployment-ready Response Conditioning Specialists.

Its output is validated specialist capacity.

Capacity allows the Pod Economy to expand.

Every Capacity Contributor owns a Production Link.

Every applicant entering through that Production Link is traced from origin to deployment.

### Capacity Production Flow

```text
Opportunity
    ↓
Application
    ↓
Training
    ↓
Sandbox
    ↓
Trial
    ↓
Certified Live Specialist
    ↓
Deployment
```

### Station Definitions

#### 1. Opportunity
A potential specialist becomes visible.

Examples:

- university outreach
- recruitment campaign
- referral
- careers event
- TikTok
- LinkedIn

Output:

Potential specialist.

#### 2. Application (applicant mode)
The individual formally applies.

The platform records:

- contributor
- recruitment campaign
- referral source
- timestamp

Output:

Applicant.

#### 3. Training (training mode)
The applicant enters the Academy.

They learn:

- Response Integrity philosophy (audited on 5 deep dives)
- topic conditioning
- platform usage
- session structure
- parent communication
- operating standards

Output:

Training-stage specialist.

#### 4. Sandbox
The trainee practices inside a controlled environment.

No live deployment.

Skills are observed.

Quality is measured.

Output:

Sandbox specialist.

#### 5. Trial
The specialist has demonstrated operational readiness and enters supervised live validation.

They may carry exactly two governed Trial families.

Each family requires nine qualifying sessions, required deterministic logs and reports, feedback received or declined, and a positive COO outcome review.

Output:

Trial specialist.

#### 6. Certified Live Specialist
The specialist satisfies all Academy standards and the full Trial validation gate.

Certification is issued only after explicit COO approval. Testimonials remain optional.

Output:

Deployable specialist.

#### 7. Deployment
The specialist is assigned learners inside a Pod.

Capacity becomes active.

Output:

Live production capacity.

### Success Metric
Capacity Production does not optimize for applications.

It optimizes for:

Deployment-ready specialists.

```text
↓

↓
Outcomes

Next Production Cycle
```

## Canonical Law
Demand without Capacity creates waiting lists.

Capacity without Demand creates idle specialists.

A Pod can only exist when both production lines arrive at the same destination at the same time.

This is the strategic heart of the model.

You are building two synchronized production systems, not one.

One manufactures demand.

One manufactures capacity.

The platform's job is to keep them in rhythm so pod creation becomes predictable rather than accidental.
