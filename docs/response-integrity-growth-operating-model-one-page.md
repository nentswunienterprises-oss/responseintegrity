# Response Integrity Growth Operating Model

One-Page Executive Brief (Implementation-Aligned)

## Strategic Identity
Response Integrity is not an online tutoring business that scales by chasing enrollments.

It is an educational production system that scales by manufacturing:

- Capacity
- Validation
- Proof
- Trust
- Pods

A pod is not the objective.
A pod is the output of a healthy operating architecture.

## Core Build Sequence
Capacity -> Validation -> Pod Economy

- Capacity: produce deployment-ready operators
- Validation: prove repeatable student outcomes and delivery quality
- Pod Economy: scale only after validation conditions are stable

## Where Academy Fits
Academy is the front door of the production system and the owner of Capacity.

Placement in the architecture:

Applications -> Academy -> Certified Capacity -> Validation Programme -> Validation Gate -> Pod Economy

Academy owns the first gating layer:

- Applicant -> Training
- Training -> SB
- SB -> Trial eligibility
- Trial -> CL graduation (Specialist Academy Validation Gate plus explicit COO decision)

Validation and Operations own the next gates:

- CL eligibility -> live assignment permission
- live assignment permission -> pod scaling permission

## The Four Laws
1. Capacity Before Demand.
2. Validation Before Scale.
3. Proof Creates Demand.
4. Every Pod Strengthens the System.

## Operating Rhythm (Live)
### Parent intake windows
- Full-year intake: 1 Nov to 31 Jan (training starts 1 Feb)
- Mid-year intake: 1 May to 31 May (training starts 1 Jun)

### Tutor certification windows
- Annual cycle: apply 1 Oct to 31 Oct, condition 1 Nov to 15 Jan, deploy 1 Feb
- Mid-year cycle: apply 1 Apr to 30 Apr, condition 1 May to 20 May, deploy 1 Jun

This rhythm is system-gated and calendar-driven, not ad hoc.

## Mode Architecture (Canonical)
Current implementation mode keys:

- applicant
- training
- sandbox
- certified_live
- watchlist
- suspended

Operational shorthand:

- Applicant
- Training
- SB (Sandbox)
- Trial
- CL (Certified Live)

Mode progression is evidence-gated:

- Docs incomplete -> applicant
- Transformation Phases complete -> sandbox eligibility
- Transformation + Session Infrastructure complete -> trial eligibility
- Trial certification case complete -> certified_live eligibility after explicit COO approval
- Drift or critical fail -> watchlist/recovery behavior
- Repeated drift threshold breach -> retraining or suspension

Trial accommodation:

Trial is now a first-class tutor lifecycle mode between Sandbox and Certified Live.

- Current implementation path: Applicant -> Training -> Sandbox -> Trial -> Certified Live
- Battle Testing and preparation readiness open Trial; they do not certify new tutors.
- Trial carries exactly two governed live families.
- Each family requires nine qualifying sessions, deterministic logs, required reports, feedback received or declined, and a positive COO outcome review.
- Certified Live requires an explicit COO final decision. Testimonials remain optional.

Target architecture cleanup:

- lifecycle mode should exclude watchlist
- watchlist should sit in a separate operational health state

## Pilot vs Commercial Billing
- Current decision: keep pilot at 9 free sessions
- Proposed alternative discussed: 4-session trial
- Operating rule: keep 9 for now, but require trial-to-live evidence gates before paid commercial conversion

## Sandbox Criteria (6 Accounts)
Before Trial, each specialist must complete the sandbox readiness pack across 6 accounts:

- Lane A, Reporting Proficiency (3 accounts): from each account, 4 weekly reports triggered and 1 monthly report triggered; report content must match drill/session logs.
- Lane B, Conditioning Execution (2 active accounts): one account must complete 1 topic end-to-end across all phases; the other account must demonstrate parallel handling of 3 active topics for 4 weeks across active Lane B accounts.
- Lane C, Control Buffer (1 untouched account): one account remains intentionally untouched for late-stage corrections, stress tests, and remediation contingency.

Sandbox -> Trial gate passes only when:

- Lane A thresholds are met and triggered
- Lane B thresholds are met and evidenced
- Lane C control buffer is preserved
- no active fail health state exists
- Specialist Academy Validation Gate signs off readiness

## Validation Gate (Scale Permission)
Scale opens only when all conditions are stable:

- service consistency
- specialist consistency
- parent trust
- measurable student improvement
- repeatable operations without founder dependency
- sufficient evidence assets (testimonials, case proof)

In software terms: state-truth before state-change.

## Pod Operating Unit
Target pod architecture:

- 12 specialists
- 36 students
- 3 students per specialist
- 9-month conditioning cycle

Current COO pod classes:

- Training Plant Pods
- Sandbox Training Pods
- Certified Live Pods
- Misaligned Pods

Misaligned pods are correction signals, not a normal steady-state class.

## The Flywheel
Capacity -> Validation -> Proof -> Trust -> Demand -> Pod Creation -> Outcomes -> Testimonials/Referrals/Data -> Stronger Capacity

Each completed cycle increases the probability of the next cycle.

## Software Rule for Leadership
Do not ask: What screen comes next?

Ask: What condition must become true before the next state is allowed?

Example:

Cannot create pod.
Reason: 9 certified specialists available.
Required: 12 certified specialists.

## North Star
Response Integrity wins when growth is no longer effort-dependent and becomes architecture-dependent.

That happens when:

- Capacity consistently produces Validation
- Validation consistently produces Trust
- Trust consistently produces Demand
- Demand consistently produces Pods
- Pods consistently strengthen Capacity
