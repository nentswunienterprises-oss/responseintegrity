# Response Integrity — Evidence Exception Doctrine (13 October 2026)

**Authoritative rule for all Specialist-facing training, Deep Dives, formative checks, assessment banks, Sandbox, Practicals, and Handover.**

## The ordinary condition

The prescribed response is observable. The method taught in Clarity and Structured Execution is unchanged by the device, lesson or timer. Log the student's actual start, structure, independence, difficulty response, recovery, pace and completion against the active conditions. Hesitation, freezing under pressure, wrong method, incomplete working, and genuine difficulty are **observed behaviour**, not missing evidence.

## Rare evidence exceptions

- `not_observed` is an **evidence status**, not a student personality, drill feature, or usual simulated outcome. It is reserved for a required observation that truly has no qualifying exposure. The operator must not create a missing dimension through an omitted question, a preventable camera view problem or poor preparation and then call that the student response. Prepare the condition correctly; restore the view *before* an opportunity if necessary. A system-designed neutral starting check is not obliged to elicit every Clarity explanation: if needed, the system opens the proper recognition check.
- `confounded` means **real observable behaviour occurred, but a concrete cause prevents an intended independent claim**. The approved exceptional student-facing example is a visible worked solution or mathematical method/step cue within reach, seen by the student before or during the independence check. Record exactly what was seen, what occurred before the cue, and which dimension became contaminated. Other clean dimensions are retained. No automatic weakness, strength, repeat, phase move, or rescue is inferred.
- A Specialist supplying mathematical help contrary to a no-support condition is logged as an actual intervention, independently of the student response. Whether it disqualifies a particular dimension is settled by the active support/evidence contract. It must not be conflated with an authored student personality incident.
- Technical connection, timer or camera failures are **real operational exceptions only if they actually occur**. They belong to equipment/readiness, interrupted-session and reserve/recovery controls. Do not author fictional display changes, random screen glitches, dropped calls, missing elicitation, camera drifting or invisible hands as normal student responses, routine Deep Dive examples or ordinary Mastery questions. Legitimate timer-reserve procedures remain independent of behavioural simulation.

## Rules for prompts and assessments

1. Prefer ordinary, credible clean, breakdown, conditional and near-stable student behaviour over edge-case training.
2. Never ask Specialists to manufacture a missing observation just to populate a field. If the prescribed question is unresolved, let RI-OS request a real qualifying check.
3. An observed worked-example cue can confound independence; correctness after seeing the cue does not restore independent proof. Affected claims are not automatically breakdown.
4. When testing evidence integrity, use the **rare worked-example contamination case** and separately preserve any earlier clean evidence. `not_observed` may be named to explain a status, but not presented as a routine fictional scenario.
5. No change of mathematical method, step order or timer contract inside a rep. The problem and prescribed method stay intact.
6. Active Specialist content and current private question banks must be **versioned and proof-verified** before promotion. Never alter a historical assessed version or reset a Specialist's earned Mastery without separately validated progression/continuity authority.
7. V1 Sandbox historical evidence remains immutable. V2 only may be recalibrated from legacy eight-outcome behavior to six ordinary patterns plus the narrowly eligible worked-example confound. Rarity is provisional simulation calibration, not an assertion about measured family incident rates.

## Current quality gate

All 20 live Deep Dive routes, imported formative questions, active approved assessment banks, retrieval and transfer banks, Sandbox V2 current narratives, and Practicals authoring must be checked against these rules. Passing mechanical tests or a count of unique narratives is not semantic authoring acceptance. An authoritative bank version is acceptable only if its options, correct keys, feedback and explanations agree with the reviewed prompt.

**Boundary:** This document does not remove the legitimate evidence-status enum or erase historical assessment attempts and bank versions. It deprecates contrived examples in current training authority, not the ability to record an actual exception if one occurs.
