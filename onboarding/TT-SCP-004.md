RESPONSE INTEGRITY (PTY) LTD
SAFEGUARDING AND CONDUCT POLICY (Response Integrity-SCP-004)

1. PURPOSE
This policy defines the safeguarding standards, conduct rules, and interaction boundaries required of all Specialists operating within the Response Integrity platform.
Response Integrity operates with minor learners in a controlled online environment.
All conduct must prioritize:
learner safety
platform integrity
controlled professional interaction
This policy is non-negotiable.

2. CORE PRINCIPLE
All Specialist conduct must remain:
professional
structured
bounded to the session environment
Specialists are not:
friends
personal support figures
private contacts
They are operators within a controlled system.

3. PLATFORM-ONLY INTERACTION
3.1 All interaction must occur within the Response Integrity platform.
The following are strictly prohibited:
private messaging with learners
contact via WhatsApp, Instagram, SMS, or any external platform
direct communication with parents outside Response Integrity channels
3.2 Specialists may not:
exchange personal contact details
accept contact requests
initiate communication outside the platform
3.3 Any attempt by a learner or parent to move communication outside the platform must be:
refused immediately
reported to Response Integrity

4. SESSION ENVIRONMENT REQUIREMENTS
4.1 All sessions must:
be conducted through Response Integrity-approved systems
be recorded in full where required
follow Response Integrity-OS structure
4.2 Specialists must maintain:
clear visual setup
appropriate environment
distraction-free working conditions
4.3 The following are prohibited during sessions:
inappropriate background environments
presence of unrelated individuals
multitasking unrelated to session execution

5. PROFESSIONAL CONDUCT WITH LEARNERS
Specialists must:
communicate clearly and respectfully
maintain emotional neutrality
enforce structure during sessions
Specialists may not:
engage in casual or personal conversations unrelated to the session
discuss personal life, relationships, or sensitive topics
form emotional dependency or familiarity with learners

6. ZERO-TOLERANCE CONDUCT
The following result in immediate suspension or termination:
any inappropriate or suggestive communication
any form of harassment, intimidation, or discrimination
sharing personal contact information
attempting to meet learners physically
any conduct that places a learner at risk
No warnings are required.

7. BOUNDARY ENFORCEMENT
7.1 Specialists must maintain strict boundaries at all times.
This includes:
no personal favours
no gifts
no off-platform assistance
no extended communication beyond scheduled sessions
7.2 Specialists must not:
position themselves as a personal support system
provide advice outside academic scope
engage in private academic support outside Response Integrity

8. SESSION RECORDING AND MONITORING
8.1 Sessions may be recorded where required.
8.2 Recordings are used for:
safeguarding
compliance
quality control
8.3 Specialists acknowledge:
they are subject to ongoing monitoring
recordings may be reviewed at any time

9. REPORTING OBLIGATIONS
9.1 Specialists must report immediately:
boundary violations
inappropriate learner behaviour
attempts to move communication off-platform
any safeguarding concerns
9.2 Failure to report is considered non-compliance.

10. DATA AND CONFIDENTIALITY
Specialists must:
protect learner data
not store or share recordings externally
not disclose learner information
All learner data remains property of Response Integrity.

11. DIGITAL CONDUCT AND PRESENCE
11.1 Specialists must:
present professionally on camera
use appropriate language at all times
11.2 The following are prohibited:
offensive language
inappropriate attire
disruptive or unprofessional behaviour

12. ENFORCEMENT
12.1 Response Integrity enforces this policy strictly.
12.2 Violations may result in:
immediate suspension
termination
payment withholding
12.3 Response Integrity decisions are final.

13. ACCEPTANCE
By accepting this policy in the Response Integrity platform, the Contractor confirms:
understanding of all safeguarding requirements
agreement to operate within strict boundaries
acceptance of zero-tolerance enforcement
